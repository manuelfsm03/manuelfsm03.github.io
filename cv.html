Southern Cone grain markets: databases of customers, producers, origination, and production capacity; portfolio reports and commercial KPIs; supply-and-demand scenarios and price projections for soybeans, corn, wheat, barley, sunflower, and sorghum.

  • Rebuilt the core grain-trade analysis notebooks: defined the canonical rule for assigning each trade to its crop year and fixed deduplication and classification bugs affecting thousands of records.
  • Built an internal Streamlit dashboard to track farmer selling, unifying several sources into a long-format model with cohort-based crop-year assignment.
  • Statistical and econometric models, including futures markets and sensitivity analysis, always benchmarked against a naive model, and reported as such when results were inconclusive.
